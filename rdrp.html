Current Studies

Reading disabilities cannot be distilled to a single, simple cause. We take an interdisciplinary approach — spanning education, pediatrics, psychology, neuroscience, and engineering — studying readers of all kinds, at every age, not just people with dyslexia.

Eye Spy

Investigating visual attention in reading — how readers deploy attention across letters and words.

Brain Pictures with Stanford RDRP

MRI studies mapping how the reading brain develops, for kids and adults across the reading spectrum.
