Research Focus

We study the interplay between brain maturation and cognitive development — how the brain's reading circuitry develops in response to education, and how targeted interventions change the brains of children with dyslexia. In collaboration with the Wu Tsai Neuroscience Institute.

NIH NICHD R01HD116845 · 2025–2030

Deep Phenotyping of Dyslexia Subtypes

Characterizing the heterogeneity within dyslexia to move toward more precise, individualized intervention.

NIH NICHD R01HD095861 · 2019–2024

Neural Mechanisms of Successful Intervention in Children with Dyslexia

A clinical trial tracking how the brain changes over the course of an intensive reading intervention.

AERDF · 2023–2025

ROAR Platform & New Assessment Components

Extending our online assessment platform with new measures, built and validated in the lab.

State of California · 2022–2025

Universal Dyslexia Screener

Developing screening tools suitable for statewide deployment.

Robertson Foundation

ROAR for Multilingual Learners in New York City

Adapting and validating reading assessment for multilingual student populations.

Klingenstein Foundation

Bridging the Lab and the Classroom

Bringing ROAR to New York and Maine to align research and classroom practice.
