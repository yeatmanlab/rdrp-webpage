Start with our own tool

ROAR@Home — a free reading check you can run yourself

ROAR is the reading assessment our lab built and validated, now rostered in 2,500+ schools. ROAR@Home is the version families can run directly: a short, game-like set of tasks your child completes on a computer, with a score report you can read and bring to a teacher or clinician.

Requires a parent or legal guardian's consent · needs a computer or tablet (no mobile) · currently U.S. only. It is a screener, not a diagnosis.
