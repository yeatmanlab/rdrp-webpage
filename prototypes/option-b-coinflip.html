Same lab. Same director. Two names for two audiences.

Both programs are directed by Prof. Jason D. Yeatman and study the same core question — how children learn to read — from different angles. Flip the coin above, or jump straight to ROAR, the assessment platform both sides helped build.

Current Research — RDRP

An interdisciplinary approach spanning education, pediatrics, psychology, neuroscience, and engineering — studying readers of all kinds, at every age.

Research Focus — BDE Lab

How the brain's reading circuitry develops in response to education, and how targeted interventions change the brains of children with dyslexia.
